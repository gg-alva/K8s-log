# Daily Learning: Logs, Metrics & Monitoring Insights

Today, I continued learning about **observability and monitoring**, with a focus on understanding the importance of **logs and metrics** in managing and maintaining systems.

### Understanding Logs & Metrics

I learned how **logs and metrics are crucial for monitoring infrastructure and applications** and how they help teams understand the health and performance of their systems.

* **Metrics** provide numerical data about system resources and performance, such as CPU utilization, memory usage, network traffic, and application performance.
* **Logs** provide detailed records of events and activities occurring within applications, servers, and other components.

By analyzing these data points, teams can identify issues, understand resource utilization, troubleshoot problems, and make informed decisions about resource management.

### Monitoring & Resource Management

I also understood how monitoring helps in **managing resources efficiently** by continuously tracking system behavior and identifying abnormal resource usage.

This is particularly important in dynamic environments such as Kubernetes, where workloads and resource requirements can change over time.
